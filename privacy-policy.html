Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Empower Church ("we," "us," "our," or "Company") operates the empowerchurch.in website. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website and use our services.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways. The information we may collect includes:

  • Contact Information: Name, email address, phone number, and other contact details when you request information about our services or submit inquiries.
  • Business Information: Church name, size, location, and specific technology needs for service planning purposes.
  • Technical Data: Information about your devices, browsers, and systems we help support through our IT services.
  • Website Usage: Information about how you interact with our website, including pages visited and time spent on our site.
  • Service Data: Information related to the technology services we provide to your organization.

3. Use of Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and provide information about our services.
  • Deliver and support the technology services you request.
  • Maintain and improve our website and service offerings.
  • Send newsletters and updates about our services (with your consent).
  • Monitor website security and prevent fraud.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Protection & Security

Your privacy and data security are paramount. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only with service providers who assist us in operating our website and providing services, and these parties are contractually obligated to maintain confidentiality.

6. Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies to enhance your experience. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, though this may affect website functionality.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal requirements).
  • Opt-out of marketing communications.

8. Children's Privacy

Our services are not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
